FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our services, process and support.

What kind of websites do you build?

We build business websites, landing pages and e-commerce websites. Every site is designed to work on mobile, tablet and desktop, and includes basic SEO.

Can you build an e-commerce website for my business?

Yes. We build online stores where customers can browse your products, add them to a cart and place orders. Before we start, we discuss your products, payment options and delivery needs.

Which digital marketing services do you offer?

We run Meta Ads and Google Ads, work on SEO and set up lead generation campaigns, including the creatives and landing pages your ads need.

How does AI video creation work?

You share your idea, product or offer. We write the story, prepare a storyboard, create the visuals with AI tools and edit the final video for the platforms you need. We also offer traditional video editing.

Do you provide website maintenance?

Yes. After launch we can keep your website updated, make content changes, fix issues and help with your domain.

What is your project process?

We follow five steps: Discover, Plan, Build, Launch and Support. We first understand your business, then plan the right solution, design and develop it, launch it and keep improving it.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size and features of your project. We share a clear timeline after our first discussion.

How can I get support after my project is delivered?

Call or message us on WhatsApp at 6369128182. We help with updates, changes and any questions after your project goes live.
